Mark the corners. Get the plan.

Stand in your kitchen and aim the ring at each floor corner in turn, going around the room in order. Your phone tracks where it is and where the floor is; you show it where the corners are. Plain walls are no problem.

How it works
  1. Tap Start corner scan and move the phone slowly until it finds the floor.
  2. Aim the ring where two walls meet the floor and tap Mark corner. Go around the room in order.
  3. If a bench hides a corner, tap Corner hidden, aim at the vertical corner edge, step sideways a metre and aim at it again.
  4. Optionally aim at the top edge of a marked wall and tap Mark ceiling.
  5. Tap Finish, check the sketch, then Save. On the next page, type in the wall lengths you measured with a tape.
