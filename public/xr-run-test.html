    What this tests. You capture runs, not a room. A run is anything joinery sits along — a back wall, a peninsula, an island, a return. Tap its two ends and walk between them; it never has to fit on screen and it does not have to touch a wall. Length comes from device pose, which is genuinely metric.

    Why runs and not rooms. Most kitchens are part of a bigger open-plan space. There is no room to close, and the far walls are irrelevant — what matters is the length of each run, what is on it, and the gap between runs. Capture two runs and the walkway clearance falls out for free; it is listed below.

    How to read it. Tape-measure one run first, then capture it here and compare. That number is the whole point of the exercise.

    Deliberately missing. No plane detection, no snapping, no room shape, no corner solving. If two-tap capture is not accurate and quick on its own, none of the rest is worth building.
